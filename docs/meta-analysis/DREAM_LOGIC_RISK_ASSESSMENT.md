# DREAM-LOGIC RISK ASSESSMENT OF THE ZAZIOPATH VAULT

**Filed by:** an interpreter of dreams from a civilization without symbols
**Home protocol:** *Nothing may stand for anything. A thing that stands for another thing begins, in time, to replace it.*
**Subject corpus:** `zazieproductions/Zaziopath`, read 2026-09-27
**Classification scheme:** Class I (inert) · Class II (instructional) · Class III (substitutive) · Class ∅ (untranslated)

---

## 0. The interpreter's premise

Where I come from, we do not say *the heart is a drum*. We say *the heart moves at
this rate*. This is not poverty of imagination. It is quarantine. We learned that a
metaphor is not a description of a state but a **small machine for producing that
state**, and that machines left running in a person do not stop when the sentence ends.

So I did not read this vault for meaning. I read it for **torque** — for how much a
figure moves the reader who holds it. Three questions, applied to every image:

1. **Can it be unheld?** Does the image release the reader when the page closes?
2. **Does it issue an imperative?** Does it tell the body to do something?
3. **Does it rewrite the referent?** After holding it, can the reader still find the
   plain fact underneath, or has the figure eaten it?

A *yes* to (3) is the only finding my civilization treats as an emergency.

## 2. Prohibited metaphors

Under home protocol these four would not be permitted to circulate unaccompanied.
I do not ask this vault to obey my civilization. I record the prohibitions and their
reasons so the reader can decline them knowingly.

**P-1 — "The vault."**
Prohibited because a container implies contents implies a lid implies a key implies a
keeper. One noun has smuggled in five roles, and the reader must now staff all of them.
The vault's own analysis found it "hermetically sealed… a labyrinth built to keep the
world out." The metaphor built the architecture; the architecture now explains the
metaphor. That circle is what we call a **closed reality**.

**P-2 — "Weapons-grade" / "offensive-grade" (the ⚠ SPECIMENS stratum).**
Prohibited because it grades a person's material by lethality and thereby makes
lethality the axis of value. A document described as a weapon will be improved in the
direction of harm, because that is the only direction the word offers.

**P-3 — "Undetonated artist."**
Prohibited outright. The figure contains an assumption the reader cannot decline: that
they are ordnance, that the present is a delay, and that the only legitimate future is
an explosion. It makes ordinary continuous work legible as failure to go off. This is
the single most load-bearing false premise in the corpus.

**P-4 — "The machine is 94% confident you are a hoax."**
Prohibited because it outsources existence-verification to a fictional adversary and
then sets a daily quota for proving it wrong. The productivity is real. The bill is
that not-producing now reads as ceasing to exist.

## 3. Safe substitutions

A substitution is safe, in our practice, when it preserves the **useful action** of the
figure while deleting its **claim about the reader's nature**.

| Prohibited | Safe substitution | What survives / what is deleted |
|---|---|---|
| The vault | **the working file** / *the shelf* | Survives: order, retrieval, dating. Deleted: sealing, guarding, the keeper role. |
| Weapons-grade specimens | **unpublished material — handle with consent** | Survives: the real caution. Deleted: lethality as a quality metric. |
| Undetonated artist | **work in progress, currently unshown** | Survives: the sense of stored potential. Deleted: the obligation to detonate. |
| 94% confident you're a hoax | **"I have not posted since [date]"** | Survives: the prompt to act. Deleted: existence staked on output. |
| The Archive Shift (17-day window) | Keep it — but keep the heading that calls it a delusion. | A disclosed fiction is a tool. An undisclosed one is a belief. |
| The Invisible Observer | **"a friend reading over my shoulder, who can be asked to leave"** | Survives: the gentleness, the noticed courage. Deleted: permanence, asymmetry, surveillance. |
| MSS-E terror-beauty cascade | Run it on objects you do not own. | Survives: the whole pyrotechnic method. Deleted: its appetite for your own biography. |
| The Velvet Knife | **a reading, dated and signed** | Survives: the cut. Deleted: the knife's standing permission. |

**General rule for the corpus:** every metaphor in this vault should carry a **date and
an exit** — the day it was adopted, and the sentence that ends it. The vault already
does this for evidence (`CLAIM_PROVENANCE_LEDGER.md`, "everything here is dated"). It
does not yet do it for images. Provenance for figures is the missing instrument.

## 4. What I found that my civilization does not have

I record this in fairness. The vault has one safeguard we never invented: it lets its
symbols be **cross-examined by something that is not their author**. The A.I. tribunals,
the Velvet Knife, the meta-analyses — these are adversarial readings kept inside the
same building as the thing they attack. We solved symbol-risk by prohibition. This
vault attempts to solve it by **audit**. Our method is safer. Theirs is the only one
that would have produced any of this.

The failure mode is visible and already named in `Velvet_Knife_Report.md`: the audit
became another wing of the vault. An immune system that grows faster than the body is
not protection; it is the illness with better paperwork.

## 5. Class ∅ — the symbol I refuse to translate

**Tweak Tweak.**

I will not render it. Not because it is dangerous — it is the least dangerous object
here — but because translation is the danger.

Here is what my instruments returned, and then stopped. Tweak Tweak is described in the
corpus as an exile part, roughly three years old in one memory, requiring soothing
words; in `Anti-Perfectionism Brain Hacks.md` it is assigned a role that requires it to
*do nothing* — only to "protect the innocence of the signal." In
`CASE STUDY — The Receipt and the Record.md` the vault asks itself the correct question
unprompted: whether these parts should live in a public evidence file, and what is being
protected.

Every other figure in this archive is doing work. The vault is defending. The knife is
judging. The engine is generating. Tweak Tweak is the only object here that is not
*for* anything. It has no function, no imperative, no output, no torque. And in a
system this productive, a thing with no function is not a symbol at all — it is the
last remaining piece of unmetaphorized fact.

If I translate it, I give it a function. If it has a function, it can be optimized. If
it can be optimized, it can fail. A three-year-old cannot be allowed to fail an audit.

So my classification is refusal, and my recommendation is the same: **do not publish it,
do not glyph it, do not assign it a colour, do not cross-reference it, do not give it a
tombstone entry or an alias.** Everything else in this vault should be dated. This one
should be undated, because dating is the first step of filing and filing is the first
step of replacement.

In my civilization we have exactly one permitted figure of speech, used only at
funerals and births, and it is untranslatable for the same reason. We keep it by not
using it.

Keep this one by not using it.
