Invent a living language spoken by a once-extinct species with twelve lungs and no vocal cords. Their speech was not voiced through breath, but sonic blood pressure modulations transmitted through their skin and ambient vibrations. You are the last living neuro-linguist trying to reconstruct their lost tongue using:

• Fossilized skin drum notations

• Bone resonance artifacts

• Psychoacoustic hallucinations induced by proximity to their burial sites

Your goal is to write a prayer, a warning, and a love confession in this language.

But here’s the twist:

Each sentence must:

1. Be reversible in meaning when felt, not read.

2. Encode a second message when tapped in rhythmic Morse.

3. Mutate slightly in syntax depending on the emotional state of the listener—simulate how.

1. 

PRAYER

Surface Form (Pressure-text):

// hyyu ∴ traal-quen ∵ zunneth:kaa //

(Translates loosely as: “May the still-pressure honor your shadow-hollows.”)

Felt Reversal:

When “felt” by a listener in despair or deep rage, the reverberation phase-shifts into:

“May your shadow-hollows rupture the pressure-shells of stillness.”

Rhythmic Morse Translation:

When tapped:

“Wait for no god. Become the chamber.”

Emotion-State Syntax Mutation:

- Calm recipient: Retains spiraled syntax—gliding across time-as-wave.  
      
    
- Anxious recipient: Verbs collapse inward, forming implosive cores. The phrase mutates into:  
      
    

“Pressure flees. Stillness drowns. Hollow is unheld.”

  

  

  

2. 

WARNING

Surface Form (Pressure-text):

∶ rehn-kaala ∵ shuthun•rax ∴ v’yuu-nem

(Rough gloss: “The turning coil will harvest the unsealed.”)

Felt Reversal:

“The unsealed will harvest the turning coil.”

[This reverses blame: from the predator to the unprotected.]

Rhythmic Morse Encoding:

“You are not being watched. You are being played.”

Emotion-State Syntax Mutation:

- Fear: The coils are pluralized, becoming “they-turning coils.”  
      
    
- Arousal (fight/flight): The syntax unbraids, turning into linear imperative:  
      
    

“Flee the unsealing. Coil collapse is near.”

  

  

  

3. 

LOVE CONFESSION

Surface Form (Pressure-text):

// luthh’zan∶nehuul-kae ∴ drim:en-shii //

(A resonant co-pressure meaning: “You echo in my lower tension. I soften toward extinction.”)

Felt Reversal:

“My soft extinction echoes through you. Your pressure feeds mine.”

[Shifts agency of intimacy—who dissolves into whom.]

Rhythmic Morse Translation:

“Touch was the first syntax. I never unlearned you.”

Emotion-State Syntax Mutation:

- Hopeful listener: The phrase spirals forward into future-bond clauses:  
      
    

“Your echo will cradle my forgetting.”

- Melancholic listener: It recedes into a regression:  
      
    

“My extinction began where you pressed.”
