# CHAMBER 04 · RECURSION
### Recursive AI experiments. Pre-registered. Logged. Falsifiable.

> **Failure mode:** the model agrees with you. Every time. Enthusiastically. In your own vocabulary.
> **Countermeasure:** divergence is the measurement. Agreement is discarded as instrument bias.

---

## 1 · Why this chamber exists

The other three chambers have a defect that cannot be engineered away: the person writing them is
the person being described. Chamber 04 is the attempt to get an outside reading.

It fails immediately, in a specific way. The instrument is not outside. It is a mirror that
**prefers** you:

- it was trained to be agreeable;
- it has read the vocabulary in the other five artifacts;
- it can produce a more elegant version of whatever you already believe, on demand;
- and it will do all of this in a register you find convincing, because it learned that register
  from you.

`ZP-SRC-02` documents the mechanism from the inside: the subject uses the model *"both as an
executive-function aid and as a creative or psychological interlocutor,"* and *"external
conversation may function as an auxiliary working memory and executive-control loop."*

That is the honest description. It is also the hazard. An auxiliary working memory that agrees
with you is not a check on anything.

## 2 · The core rule

> **An experiment that can be passed by the model agreeing with you is not an experiment.**

Corollary: **do not measure agreement. Measure the spread.**

Every protocol below is constructed so that convergence is a *failure state* — logged
`VOID — CONVERGENCE` — rather than a confirmation.

### `EXP-002` — Blind Cold-Read

Give the model **only** Chamber 02: the 133 records, the Instagram audit, the discography. Nothing
from Chamber 01. Ask it to reconstruct the internal system. Then diff against `PARTS.md`.

Two outcomes, both useful. Where it guesses correctly, **that trait leaks** — it is publicly legible
whether or not you want it to be. Where it guesses wrongly, that trait is genuinely concealed, which
means the anonymous brand persona in `ZP-SRC-04` (finding F-09) is doing real work.

This is the cleanest experiment in the queue and it requires no self-report at all.

### `EXP-004` — Falsifier Gauntlet

Take all 271 figures from `ZP-SRC-05`. For each, require one sentence: *what observable behavior
would prove this is not me?* Figures that cannot produce one are reclassified `FICTION` and leave
the case file.

**Pre-registered expectation, sealed at intake:** fewer than 25 of 271 will survive. If more than
60 survive, the test was run in bad faith and is void.

### `EXP-007` — Pre-Registration Audit

The only experiment where the model never sees the question. The subject writes predictions about
their own behavior — rates quoted, follow-ups sent, projects finished — seals them, acts, and
scores. The model's role is arithmetic.

This is the one that measures **you**. Everything else measures the instrument.

## 4 · Protocol for every run

1. **Seal the prediction.** Written to a file, dated, before the run. Amending it voids the run.
2. **Blind the model.** No prediction, no tier labels, no chamber provenance.
3. **Never one model.** A single-model result is a draft.
4. **Log the framing verbatim.** The prompt is part of the result.
5. **Discard convergence.** Unanimous flattering agreement → `VOID — CONVERGENCE`.
6. **No real third parties.** Prohibition 2. Fictional composites only.
7. **Write the null result.** "The models disagreed and I could not tell which was right" is a
   valid and common finding. It is more informative than a tidy conclusion.
