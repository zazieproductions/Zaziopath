## 4 · Standing rules

1. **Date every snapshot.** Undated metrics are inadmissible.
2. **Embarrassing number first.** Always.
3. **Never revise a snapshot.** Supersede it. `SIG-2026-08-19` stays exactly as retrieved even when
   the next one is better.
4. **Perception, not conduct.** A finding means an average viewer could reasonably form an
   impression — not that the impression is true.
5. **No diagnosis, no motive-inference.** Inherited from the source, non-negotiable.
6. **The gap is the data.** Chamber 02 vs Chamber 01 is the leak rate. That comparison is
   `EXP-002` and it is the single most informative experiment in the queue.
