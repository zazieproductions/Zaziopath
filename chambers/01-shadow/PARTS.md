## 2 · `Q-01` — the open question

> Are these two lists the same eleven things described twice, or is the analytic list describing
> parts the lived ones have never met?

**Status:** OPEN · untested · no prediction sealed.

**Why it matters.** If they are the same list, the vault has two vocabularies for one system and
should collapse them — carrying both is exactly the "human mood board" drift `ZP-SRC-05` §2.0.9
warns about. If they are *not* the same list, then five parts have names the subject uses and six
do not, which means six parts are being described from the outside by an instrument and never
spoken to.

**Candidate crosswalk — T2, deliberately unconfirmed:**

| Lived | Analytic | Confidence | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ma$imillion Munnytown | The Tiny Sovereign | moderate | Both tiny, both princely, both demand reorganisation of the room |
| Ma$imillion Munnytown | The Undervalued Prince | moderate | Competing candidate. Same problem. |
| Tweak Tweak | The Forensic Child | low | Tweak Tweak is shame-bearing; the Forensic Child is analytical. May be different parts. |
| Hubris | The Unseen Auteur | low | Hubris protects status; the Unseen Auteur wants recognition. Adjacent, not identical. |
| Tuffy Bunnytown | — | — | **No analytic counterpart proposed.** The sacred fool has no shadow-analytic twin. |
| Babyheart | — | — | **No analytic counterpart proposed.** |

Two lived parts have no analytic twin. That asymmetry is itself a result and is logged as such.

**FALSIFIER:** If, in five consecutive parts-conversations, the subject refers to an analytic name
without first using a lived name, the two lists are functionally separate and this crosswalk is
void.
