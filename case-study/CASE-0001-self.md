## 1 · Presenting pattern

Two patterns that look separate and behave as one loop:

- **`P-06` — The achievement discount.** Completed work does not register as completed. Internal
  goalposts move; the accomplishment is spent before it is felt.
- **`P-11` — The underprice reflex.** Quotes are set low, revisions offered unlimited, extras added
  unasked — because the immediate fear of losing the opportunity outweighs the long-term arithmetic.

The loop: work finishes → it does not register → worth is unproven → the next opportunity must be
secured at any price → the price is low → low price attracts clients who demand more and validate
less → worth remains unproven.

`ZP-SRC-02` already names the closing move: *"Low pricing may attract clients who demand more labor
and provide less validating feedback, reinforcing the original insecurity."*

**This is the case because it is the one pattern pair with a hard, unfakeable record attached.**
Everything else in the vault is inference. This one has ISRCs.

## 3 · Competing explanations

Minimum three. **The flattering one is written last.**

### H1 · Rejection sensitivity, not price ignorance
The subject knows the market rate. `ZP-SRC-02` records high financial and scope caution, escrow
requests, and forensic evaluation of every offer. The underpricing is not a knowledge deficit. It
is the cost of avoiding a specific outcome — being declined — and the discount is what the
avoidance is priced at.
**Supported by:** E-05, E-07, E-09.
**Would be weakened by:** a rate history showing prices rising steadily with experience.

### H2 · The portfolio is genuinely heterogeneous, and breadth suppresses rate
Eleven adjacent service identities make the offer hard to price. A buyer who cannot categorise the
service cannot justify a premium for it. The discount is a legibility problem, not an emotional one.
**Supported by:** E-02, E-08, `P-12`.
**Would be weakened by:** high rates being achieved on the *narrowest*, most legible service while
the broad ones stay low. If rate tracks legibility rather than fear, H2 wins.
**This is the most falsifiable hypothesis in the case and it has not been tested.**

### H3 · Output and revenue are decoupled by channel, not by psychology
200 tracks across 2019–2026, with 35 carrying no ISRC and 12 of 16 compilation entries
Bandcamp-only. A substantial share of the catalogue sits in distribution channels that do not pay
at scale. The income figure may reflect **where** the work was released rather than **how the
subject feels about it.**
**Supported by:** E-01, E-04, and the workbook's own note that "—" means Bandcamp/netlabel-only.
**Would be weakened by:** ISRC-verified work also failing to generate revenue.
**If H3 is correct, a large part of this case is a distribution problem wearing a psychology
costume** — and the intervention is wrong.

### H4 · *(the flattering one, last, as required)*
The subject is early, unusually prolific for their age and infrastructure, and underpricing is a
rational land-grab in an experimental niche where the audience is small and the work is
difficult to categorise. `ZP-SRC-02` supports this: *"Zazie may be able to produce at a level that
currently appears disproportionate to age, income, and formal infrastructure."*
**Why it is last:** it is almost certainly partly true, it is the most comfortable, and it is the
one that requires nothing to change.

**No single hypothesis is adopted.** H2 and H3 are cheaper to test than H1 and would change the
intervention entirely. They go first.

## 4 · Intervention

**Pre-committed before any result exists.** One protocol, from Chamber 03.

> **`CASE-0001-I1`** — For the next three inbound scoring or production inquiries, quote at the
> stated floor and **hold it through one revision request** without discounting, adding unrequested
> extras, or extending revision limits.
>
> Protocol: `ZP-SRC-05` §23.2 (*before accepting a low-budget creative project*).
> Pattern tested: `P-11`.
> Falsifier (from `patterns/INDEX.md`): the quote is sent at or above floor and held through one
> revision request without a discount.

**Pre-registered outcome, sealed 2026-08-27:**

```
Expected: at least 2 of 3 inquiries are lost.
If 3 of 3 are lost      → P-11 is under-priced fear of a real market signal. Revisit H2/H3.
If 0 of 3 are lost      → the fear is not calibrated to reality. H1 strengthens. P-11 stays ACTIVE.
If 1 of 3 is lost       → the base case. The discount was buying nothing.
```

The prediction is uncomfortable, which is how you know it is a real prediction rather than a
hope.

**Prerequisite — must run first:** close `CASE-0001-G1`. Build the rate history. Without it, this
case has no baseline and the intervention cannot be scored.
