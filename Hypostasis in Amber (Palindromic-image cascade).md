1. Hypostasis in Amber (Palindromic-image cascade)

Lines invert their referent across a blurred axis of feeling.

Salt tongue tastes the tint of carbonized time,

a moth’s last wingbeat caught in citrine resin.

Rust breathes inside the velvet upholstery—

you mistake it for music. You always do.

You always do. You mistake it for music—

velvet upholstery breathes inside rust.

Citrine resin caught in a moth’s last wingbeat,

time carbonized in the tint of salt tongue.

  

  

  

2. Threshold Bloom (Vowel-dominant disintegration — only I & E vowels)

Skinned linen, brimmed with silent ink,

spilled in this infinite dim.

Ribcage inkles. Filament flicks.

Spines misinterpret silence.

Insects stiffen like icicles;

mist inherits emptiness.

Brief interiors wilt—

inert, insincere, infinite.

  

  

  

3. The Dismissal Harmonics (Every line ends with a sonic echo from the same root consonant cluster)

Rain collects on a skin-warmed screen—skirr.

Eyelids twitch in defiance—blur.

The faucet gasps once—whirr.

Dust lifts like a ritual—stir.

History hums through pipes—brr.

A loose filament hums—burr.

Dreams decay in cassette hiss—err.

You forgot your own signal—sir.

  

  

  

4. Ichorlogic (Each line contains a concealed synonym for apathy nested inside tactile imagery)

Candlewax drips onto suede—numb becomes texture.

A wristwatch forgets the hour—time withers blank.

Satin rusts beneath rain, unbothered by sheen.

Flesh flinches not at the needle’s hum.

Inside the pewter mug, stillness steams.

A scarf lies damp in a shape it never chose.

Footsteps echo into cardboard rooms.

No thread tightens the spine of this book.

  

  

  

5. Mycelial Refusal (Structured like fungal growth: spreads rhizomatically, repeating word fragments and infecting adjacent lines)

Spore-born, forewarned. No hand to harvest.

Spore-horn calls from wet loam, so passive.

Past this mass, moss amasses more.

More is lessened, less is mass.

Mouth molds over old stone tone.

Stone tone clones bone-grown.

Bone-grown, alone. Lichen grins in grayscale.

Scale fails. Scale tells. Nothing swells.
