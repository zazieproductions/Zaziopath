## How to read this file

Every entry carries a falsifier **written at intake**. That ordering is the whole method: a pattern
you cannot disprove is not a pattern, it is a self-image.

```
STATUS:  ACTIVE     — falsifier untested, window open
         CONTESTED  — falsifier partially fired; under review
         RETIRED    — disproved. Stays in the file, dated, with what killed it.
         FICTION    — could not produce a falsifier. Reclassified. Not evidence.
```

**Tier** follows `docs/METHOD.md §2`. All fourteen seeds below originate as `T1` — the source
classifies them as `direct_evidence` in `ZP-SRC-02`, but they are inferences drawn from
conversation, not retrievable records, so they enter here as T1 pending re-evidence and are marked
accordingly.

## The fourteen

Seeded verbatim from the `direct_evidence` list in `ZP-SRC-02` → *Hidden Patterns You Notice*.

### `P-01` — The machinery hunt
**Statement:** Repeatedly returns to hidden mechanisms — white-label agencies, ghost production,
paid editorial, abandoned-business flipping, narrative seeding, trolling-as-a-service, affiliate
funnels, autonomous agents, dark-web markets, prestige manufacturing.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · **First seen:** pre-2026 · **Last seen:** 2026
**FALSIFIER:** If, across 30 consecutive days, not one unsolicited inquiry into a hidden mechanism
is made, and a revenue decision is taken on public-rate evidence alone, the pattern is not
operative.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE · window closes 2026-09-26

---

### `P-02` — The visibility demand
**Statement:** Repeatedly asks how to become more visible, searchable, envied, culturally magnetic,
or impossible to ignore.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · cross-checks `ZP-SRC-04` F-09 (total anonymity)
**FALSIFIER:** If a discoverability improvement is offered and declined in favour of an unindexed
release, twice, the pattern is weaker than stated.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** In direct tension with `ZP-SRC-04` F-09 — the public persona carries no face and no
personal name. The demand for searchability is real; the surface refuses it. That contradiction is
a finding, not an inconsistency.

---

### `P-03` — The asymmetric bet
**Statement:** Repeatedly seeks low-cost, high-upside, fast-scaling paths while simultaneously
asking for skeptical reality checks.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02`
**FALSIFIER:** If a slow, certain, low-ceiling revenue channel is chosen over a fast uncertain one
when both are available, twice.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE

---

### `P-04` — The oscillation
**Statement:** Repeatedly moves between grand ambition and fears of being invisible, behind,
underpaid, or not legitimate.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · consistent with `ZP-SRC-05` §1.2 (vulnerable grandiosity)
**FALSIFIER:** If an emotionally neutral middle position is described and sustained across a week —
not "I'm fine," but specific, dated, unbothered — the pattern is over-stated.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** `ZP-SRC-02` speculative tier already frames this as "little emotionally neutral middle
ground." That is T2. It stays T2.

---

### `P-05` — The persona metabolism
**Statement:** Repeatedly uses personas, lore, parody, cute animals, and theatrical language to
metabolise shame, entitlement, envy, dependency, and ambition.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · five named parts, see `chambers/01-shadow/PARTS.md`
**FALSIFIER:** If a shame event is processed in plain first-person language without a persona,
three times, the persona is a preference rather than a mechanism.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** The most valuable pattern in the file and the one most easily mistaken for decoration.

---

### `P-06` — The achievement discount
**Statement:** Repeatedly discounts completed achievements and needs them reconstructed from
emails, releases, credits, and public evidence.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · **Hard control:** `ZP-SRC-06` — 200 tracks, 165
ISRC-verified, 2019–2026
**FALSIFIER:** If an accomplishment is stated in plain counts without a diminishing clause
("only," "just," "nothing major") in five consecutive instances.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** The only pattern in this file with an unfakeable counter-record already on disk.
Chamber 02 §3 is that record. It is a control condition, not encouragement.

---

### `P-07` — The conversion reflex
**Statement:** Repeatedly turns emotional states into creative prompts, marketing concepts,
narrative devices, or aesthetic systems.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02`
**FALSIFIER:** If an emotional state is allowed to stay unproductive — no output, no concept, no
aesthetic — for seven consecutive days without distress about the absence of output.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** `ZP-SRC-02` names the risk directly: *"the most hidden risk is turning every
vulnerability into content before it has been privately cared for."* Prohibition 3 is the
structural answer.

---

### `P-08` — The transparency wish
**Statement:** Repeatedly wants to know what others secretly think, envy, conceal, manipulate, or
profit from.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02`
**FALSIFIER:** If a collaborator's unstated motive is explicitly *not* investigated when the
opportunity arises, three times.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE · **see `chambers/03-stewardship/COUNTERPATTERNS.md §4`** — this pattern is the
primary false-positive generator.

---

### `P-09` — The selective delegation
**Statement:** Repeatedly seeks automation or delegation for routine work while preserving personal
control over taste, identity, and artistic judgment.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02`
**FALSIFIER:** If a taste-adjacent decision is delegated once and left alone.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** Not a contradiction. A boundary that holds in one domain and not another is a boundary.

---

### `P-10` — The double appetite
**Statement:** Repeatedly gravitates toward experimental horror and media-decay aesthetics while
also craving playful, unserious, low-stakes creation.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · `ZP-SRC-06` contains both registers
**FALSIFIER:** If the playful register is produced publicly, unrebranded as art, twice.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** `ZP-SRC-02` states that low-stakes play "is maintenance for the creative system and a
counterweight to status-driven production." Not a side hobby. Load-bearing.

---

### `P-11` — The underprice reflex
**Statement:** Repeatedly underprices or overoffers because the immediate fear of losing an
opportunity outweighs long-term business logic.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` — difficulty charging above ~$50 in some contexts;
unlimited revisions offered; a few hundred dollars per month against a broad portfolio
**FALSIFIER:** If a quote is sent at or above the stated floor and held through one revision
request without a discount.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** The single most directly testable pattern in the file. It is also the one with the
clearest cost. **This should be tested first.**

---

### `P-12` — The adjacent-identity test
**Statement:** Repeatedly tests multiple adjacent career identities rather than accepting a single
narrow label.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` · `ZP-SRC-01` brand identity: *Underground Polymath*
**FALSIFIER:** If one professional label is used without qualification across ten consecutive
public statements.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** `ZP-SRC-02` reads this both ways — "breadth is not merely scattered; it creates a genuine
cross-media competitive advantage" and "breadth can blur positioning." Both are T1. The conflict is
logged, not resolved.

---

### `P-13` — The discovery loop
**Statement:** Repeatedly asks for increasingly obscure examples, suggesting that the pleasure of
discovery itself can become a loop.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02`
**FALSIFIER:** If a known example is accepted as sufficient three times when a more obscure one was
available.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** This pattern is currently operating on the vault itself. Reading this file and wanting a
fifteenth pattern is `P-13`.

---

### `P-14` — The click-then-analyse
**Statement:** Repeatedly explores suspicious links or products despite recognising the risk, then
seeks retrospective mechanism analysis.
**Tier:** T1 · **Evidence:** `ZP-SRC-02` — admitted clicking questionable links repeatedly because
curiosity overrode caution
**FALSIFIER:** If a flagged link is declined at the point of curiosity, three times, without
subsequent analysis of what was declined.
**STATUS:** ACTIVE
**Note:** The only pattern here with a direct safety cost. It is not merely epistemic.
