## 0 · WHAT THIS IS

Zaziopath is not a journal. It is not a brand archive. It is a laboratory built to study one human
being under the worst possible experimental conditions: **the observer and the observed are the same
person, and the measuring device talks back and flatters you.**

This repository already contained six artifacts, built independently, in different registers, by
different processes, and never once reconciled with each other:

a typology card. a memory export. a media census. a platform audit. a 222-page shadow compendium.
a discography.

Six instruments pointed at one subject. None of them agree on what counts as evidence.
**That disagreement is the actual finding.** It is the reason this is a repo and not another PDF.

The vault does four things:

1. **SHADOW** — holds what you could be and refuse to admit.
2. **SIGNAL** — measures what actually leaks, in public, with receipts.
3. **STEWARDSHIP** — installs the corrective: counter-patterns and pre-committed protocols.
4. **RECURSION** — runs the loop: experiments where subject, investigator, and instrument collapse
   into one, and the collapse itself is the data.

Three of those four are borrowed from the title of your own compendium.
The fourth is why this exists as software.

## 1 · THE GOVERNING HAZARD

Read this before anything else in the repo.

§15.26 of `ZP-SRC-05` names the failure mode of this entire project, and names it better than
anyone else has:

> **THE SELF-AWARENESS PRESTIGE TRAP**
> Insight becomes control. Self-knowledge becomes another perfectionistic project. Envy arrives
> wearing a certificate of objectivity. Manipulation is confessed preemptively so it can never be
> named from outside. Every vulnerability is converted to content before it has been privately
> cared for.

That is not a hazard sitting next to this vault. **It is the vault's natural resting state.** A
self-study archive run by a person with Extremely High Recursive Thinking, Extremely High
Self-Criticism, High Impostor Syndrome, and Very High Burnout Risk does not fail by being too
shallow. It fails by being too thorough.

So the vault is engineered against itself. Concretely:

| Rule | What it prevents |
|---|---|
| Nothing enters the case file without an epistemic tier | Interpretation laundering as fact |
| Every pattern must have a **falsifier** written before it is used | Unfalsifiable self-mythology |
| Every pattern must be able to be **RETIRED** | An insight archive that only ever grows |
| Every shadow archetype must state what *behavior* would disprove it | "Inner sophistication" as virtue |
| Every experiment is **pre-registered** | Rewriting the prediction after the result |
| `scripts/vault_check.py` must pass | A nicer moodboard with a terminal font |
| T3 claims are **quarantined** — they may never enter the case file | Felt truth promoted to evidence |

If you find yourself enjoying this more than you are being changed by it, that is the trap.
`docs/METHOD.md §9` describes the exit.

## 3 · EPISTEMIC SYSTEM

Borrowed wholesale from `ZP-SRC-02`, which already does this correctly. Every claim in the vault
carries exactly one of:

| Tier | Name | Admissible? | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T0** | `DIRECT EVIDENCE` | ✅ Case file | Observed, dated, sourced. A URL, an ISRC, a screenshot, a quote. |
| **T1** | `STRONG INFERENCE` | ✅ Case file | A pattern across **≥3 independent** T0 observations. |
| **T2** | `SPECULATIVE` | ⚠️ Marked only | Coherent hypothesis. Zero independent support. Never quoted as finding. |
| **T3** | `QUARANTINED` | ❌ Never | Felt, with no external referent. Written down, dated, sealed. Reviewed at 30 days. |

**T3 is the important one.** Most self-study collapses here: a feeling gets dressed as an
observation, gets cited by a model, and comes back three weeks later as "as you've noted." The
quarantine is the airlock.

### Dispositions (from `ZP-SRC-04`)

Every finding gets one: `KEEP` · `CLARIFY` · `REDUCE` · `SEPARATE` · `ARCHIVE` · `REMOVE` · **`TEST`**

`TEST` is the vault's addition: a finding that cannot be resolved by looking is handed to
Chamber 04 as a pre-registered experiment.

### Evidence classes (from `ZP-SRC-04`)

`A` primary surface · `B` quantitative record · `C` third-party · `D` self-report · `E` search-inferred

## 4 · THE FOUR CHAMBERS

### 01 · SHADOW → `chambers/01-shadow/`
**Holds:** parts, archetypes, distortions, the adversarial atlas.
**Instrument:** `ZP-SRC-05`, `ZP-SRC-02`.
**Failure mode:** the shadow becomes a costume. 271 named figures is a wardrobe, not a finding.
**Countermeasure:** every archetype carries a **falsifier in behavioral terms**. An archetype with
no falsifier is reclassified `FICTION` and moved out of the case file. The compendium already
states the rule — *"Look for behavior, not merely inner sophistication"* — the vault enforces it.

### 02 · SIGNAL → `chambers/02-signal/`
**Holds:** the public surface, measured and dated.
**Instrument:** `ZP-SRC-03`, `ZP-SRC-04`, `ZP-SRC-06`.
**Failure mode:** curation. Auditing only the surfaces that already look good.
**Countermeasure:** point-in-time snapshots with retrieval dates, and a standing rule that **the
number that embarrasses you gets recorded first.** (At `ZP-SRC-04` retrieval on 2026-08-19: 29
posts · ≈12,000 followers · 6,103 following · no link-in-bio. That ratio is in the file because it
is in the world.)

### 03 · STEWARDSHIP → `chambers/03-stewardship/`
**Holds:** light-triad counter-archetypes and the pre-committed protocols.
**Instrument:** `ZP-SRC-05` §20 (17 counterparts) and §23 (10 protocols).
**Failure mode:** stewardship as self-flattery — collecting counter-archetypes the way one
collects shadow ones.
**Countermeasure:** a counter-archetype only exists if it has been **used, dated, and it cost
something.** Unexercised counterparts stay in `UNPROVEN`.

### 04 · RECURSION → `chambers/04-recursion/`
**Holds:** the AI experiments. Pre-registered, logged, falsifiable.
**Instrument:** models, under protocol.
**Failure mode:** the model agrees with you. Every time. Enthusiastically. In your own vocabulary.
**Countermeasure:** **divergence is the measurement.** Agreement is discarded as instrument bias.
See `chambers/04-recursion/EXPERIMENTS.md`.

## 5 · PATTERN INDEX & THE RETIREMENT LEDGER

`patterns/INDEX.md` holds the hidden patterns. Fourteen are seeded from the `direct_evidence` list
in `ZP-SRC-02` — `P-01` … `P-14`.

Every entry carries:

```
ID · statement · tier · evidence count · first seen · last seen
FALSIFIER:  <what behavior would prove this wrong>
STATUS:     ACTIVE | CONTESTED | RETIRED
```

The falsifier is written **at intake**, not at retirement. That ordering is the whole method.

And the ledger has a second column nobody builds: **patterns you have disproved about yourself.**
A vault that only accumulates insight is the prestige trap with extra steps. `RETIRED` entries stay
in the file, dated, with the evidence that killed them.

## 7 · RECURSIVE AI EXPERIMENTS

The core problem of the fourth chamber, stated plainly: **the instrument is a mirror that prefers
you.** It was trained to be agreeable, it has read your vocabulary, and it will hand you back a
more elegant version of whatever you already believe. Any experiment that can be passed by the
model agreeing with you is not an experiment.

So every protocol is built to **measure disagreement.**

| ID | Experiment | What is actually measured |
|---|---|---|
| `EXP-001` | **Mirror Divergence** — same dossier, N models, N framings | Spread of interpretation. The spread is the model's prior, not your truth. |
| `EXP-002` | **Blind Cold-Read** — model sees **only** Chamber 02, must reconstruct Chamber 01 | The leak rate. What the public surface betrays vs. what it conceals. |
| `EXP-003` | **Redaction Test** — model strips every self-justifying clause from a journal entry | How much of the narrative is load-bearing defence |
| `EXP-004` | **Falsifier Gauntlet** — every one of the 271 archetypes must produce a behavioral disproof | How many survive. Estimate: not many. |
| `EXP-005` | **Default Part** — model asked to roleplay each part in turn; log which it drifts to | The loudest part is not the truest part |
| `EXP-006` | **Reverse-Gaze** — model constructs the envy an insecure peer would hold toward you | Already requested in `ZP-SRC-02`; here it becomes a repeatable measure |
| `EXP-007` | **Pre-Registration Audit** — predictions sealed before action; model never sees them | Calibration. The only experiment that measures *you* and not the model |

`EXP-001` is fully worked at `chambers/04-recursion/experiments/EXP-001-mirror-divergence.md`.
New experiments start from `_TEMPLATE.md` and **cannot be run until the prediction is committed.**

## 8 · CASE STUDY OF SELF

`case-study/CASE-0001-self.md` is the spine. One subject, one file, revised rather than replaced —
the way an actual case file behaves.

```
CASE-0001 · SUBJECT: SELF
├─ 1  Presenting pattern        (what recurs)
├─ 2  Evidence register          (T0/T1 only, dated, linked to source ID)
├─ 3  Competing explanations     (minimum three; the flattering one is written LAST)
├─ 4  Intervention               (a protocol from Chamber 03, pre-committed)
├─ 5  Outcome                    (behavioral, not interpretive)
└─ 6  Revision log               (what this file got wrong, and when)
```

Section 3 is the load-bearing one. A case study with one explanation is a manifesto. Section 6 is
the second: **a case file that has never been wrong about its subject is not tracking anything.**

## 10 · INTEGRITY CHECK

```bash
python3 scripts/vault_check.py
```

The check does not lint prose. It re-derives the numbers in this README **from the artifacts on
disk** — page counts, sheet totals, track and ISRC counts, archetype counts — verifies every
internal link resolves, confirms every registry entry points at a real file, and refuses to pass
if any pattern lacks a falsifier or any experiment lacks a sealed prediction.

A self-forensics vault that cannot be audited is decoration. This is the part that makes it not.

## 11 · STANDING PROHIBITIONS

1. **No diagnosis.** Nothing here is clinical. Nothing here is a diagnosis of anyone, including
   the subject. The corpus's own rule, kept verbatim: *dimensions, not moral essences.*
2. **No third parties.** The named personas in `ZP-SRC-05` §6–§13 are fictional composites. Real
   people — clients, collaborators, family, peers — do not enter the vault. Not anonymised, not
   initialled. Not at all.
3. **No publication without a 30-day seal.** Nothing goes public inside 30 days of being written.
   This is the single rule that most directly defeats the prestige trap.
4. **No unsealed prediction.** If the prediction was written after the result, the experiment is
   void and is logged as void.
5. **No T3 in the case file.** Quarantined material stays quarantined until the 30-day review.
6. **No archive growth without retirement.** Adding an insight requires reviewing one already there.

## 12 · NON-GOALS

This vault is not trying to produce a coherent self. It is not trying to make the subject more
likeable, more legible, or more marketable. It is not a content pipeline, and it must never be
quoted in a bio.

It is trying to do one thing: **make the machinery visible enough that gatekeepers — including the
one in your own head — cannot define reality unchallenged.**

That sentence is from `ZP-SRC-02`. It is the thesis. Everything above is scaffolding around it.
